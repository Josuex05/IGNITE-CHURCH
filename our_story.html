OUR STORY

Pastor Jorge

Senior Pastor

Jorge Caban is the Senior pastor of Ignite Church in Newark, New Jersey. Pastor Jorge started off with a youth movement called Ignite Revolution in 2011 where over 1,000 youth would come to encounter Christ every month. Two years later Ignite Church was birthed. Pastor Jorge along with his wife, Zulma, have used their testimonies to change the lives of so many people at Ignite. They have cultivated a culture of love and compassion for the community around them. Pastor Jorge also attended Grace Hill Bible College where he received his degree in Theology. Pastor Jorge is also a father of 2, Sergio and Elleniz.

Ed and Alice Padilla

Associate Pastor

Ed and Alice have been serving with Ignite Church for the last 6 years. Just last year, in 2018, they were recognized as the Associate Pastors of the house as well. They help run our Connect Team and our iGroup teams. They also have taken their 24 years of marriage and helped put together our Marriage Ministry, helping couples learn different ways to keep Jesus at the center of their marriages. They have been rocks for our Senior Pastors to lean on since the very beginning. Their goal is to help push Ignite Church to reaching its full potential.

Mike

Worship Director

Mike has been with Ignite Church since it's opening service in January of 2013. He has taken on the responsibility of being our Worship Director. His goal is to help the people of our church connect with God through music. Mike focuses on bringing an understanding of what worship is and why it is important. Mike also helps produce and write music for Ignite Live.
